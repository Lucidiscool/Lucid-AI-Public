Run Lucid on your PC

Open PowerShell in your Lucid-AI-Public folder. With its Python dependencies, local model, and runtime/cloudflared.exe already installed, run:

.\start-local-host.cmd

The launcher privately prompts for your admin passcode. Keep the terminal and PC running. Paste the HTTPS address it prints below. The server checks it before switching visitors.

Use the same passcode as your cloud Space for PC chat review. Do not save it in the repository. Stop the launcher with Ctrl+C after switching back to cloud.
